Wizznic for AmigaOS 3.x: what is whose
======================================

The game
--------
Wizznic! 1.1, (C) 2009-2015 Jimmy Christensen (DusteD),
https://github.com/DusteDdk/Wizznic. The program is GPL 3 or later
(COPYING).

The data in this drawer
-----------------------
Upstream's doc/media-licenses.txt (here: Media-Licences) says: all media
are licensed under GPLv3. It names the stock material the pictures and
sounds were made from (DeviantArt textures and brushes, the "Latin Runes"
font from FontStruct, sounds by Bart Kelsey, Michel Baradari and others)
and the music, by Sean Hawk (seanhawk.dk), a friend of the author's.
All of it comes with the game, from its author, under GPL 3.

- data/: menus, fonts, pictures, sounds and the menu music: GPL 3.
- packs/000_wizznic, 001_wizznic ("Wizznic! 1" and "2", by DST/VMD, the
  author's team) and 099_wizznic-silver (by DusteD): levels, themes,
  sounds and Sean Hawk's music: GPL 3.
- The sounds and music are upstream's Ogg Vorbis files converted to WAV
  for this drawer (16-bit PCM effects, IMA ADPCM music, 22050 Hz mono):
  the same works, still GPL 3.
- The icons are drawn from the game's own data/wmicon.png (GPL 3).

Not in this drawer: packs 200 to 208 ("Nes levels" by bMan, "Puzznic! S.4"
to "S.14" by KML). They are the levels of Taito's Puzznic typed in by
fans, with no licence of their own, so they are not shipped.

The Amiga port
--------------
amiga/ in the source (the SDL_image and SDL_mixer shims, the path
wrapper, the screens fix, the stack, the build and the sound converter):
the AmiCradle Team, GPL 3 or later, like Wizznic. Parts come from the
Team's OpenLieroX, Commander Genius and Seven Kingdoms ports.

Linked in: SDL 1.2 as sdl12-compat (zlib licence) with SDL 2's program
half (zlib licence), from the OpenGPU developer kit; libpng (the libpng
licence) and zlib (zlib licence); libnix from GFOpenAmigaGCC.
